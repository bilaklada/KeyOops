# KeyOops 0.2.1 beta 4 (build 9)

Quit KeyOops, open **KeyOops-build-9-macOS-universal.dmg**, and drag KeyOops into Applications. Choose Replace and launch the installed copy. The app supports macOS 14 or later on Intel and Apple silicon.

This beta is ad-hoc signed and has no Apple notarization. See the [first-launch guide](https://github.com/bilaklada/KeyOops/blob/v0.2.1-beta.4/docs/releases/0.2.0-beta.1.md). Enable Accessibility and Input Monitoring for /Applications/KeyOops.app, then choose Suggest or Automatic in its menu.

## What changed

Recognized source editors and verified typing fields without selection-replacement support can now show a preview in both Suggest and Automatic modes. KeyOops leaves their text and keyboard shortcuts alone. The menu can also show the wording when caret bounds are unavailable.

Secure inputs, credential prompts and recognized terminals remain excluded. The host must expose the caret, bounded text ranges and enough information to isolate the field and document. Automated live-edit and inline preview checks passed. Live operation in the VS Code Codex prompt and Gmail remains unverified.
